About Our Workshop

Established with an unrelenting passion for high performance and automotive engineering excellence, Starboy Automotives transforms everyday vehicles into track-ready performance machines. Based out of Pretoria, Gauteng, our facility was created to bridge the gap between street reliability and motor racing dynamics.

Our workshop houses state-of-the-art diagnostic equipment, custom fabrication tools, and an all-wheel-drive dynamometer. Whether you are seeking a refined ECU recalibration, bespoke exhaust fabrication, or a complete engine overhaul, our team approaches every vehicle with meticulous attention to detail.

Our Journey & Philosophy

What started as a small passion project among track enthusiasts has grown into a premier automotive tuning house. We believe that true performance requires more than just adding horsepower—it demands a balance between power, handling, stopping power, and heat management.

Every component we install and every custom map we flash undergoes testing to ensure optimum reliability under real-world performance conditions.

Our Core Pillars

  1. Precision Engineering: Uncompromising accuracy in ECU calibration, component clearance tolerances, and custom structural fabrication.
  2. Technological Innovation: Continuous investment in cutting-edge diagnostic software, telemetry tools, and modern dyno equipment.
  3. Track-Proven Passion: Built by active motorsport enthusiasts, ensuring every customer vehicle benefits from real track-tested experience.
  4. Customer Transparency: Providing detailed data logs, clear mechanical consultations, and transparent performance metrics before and after every build.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Custom Calibration: We do not use generic off-the-shelf software files; all tuning is tailored on-site for your specific setup.
  • Bespoke Fabrication: In-house TIG welding for titanium and stainless steel exhaust systems, intake piping, and custom intercoolers.
  • End-to-End Project Management: From part sourcing to final dyno verification, we manage complex engine and suspension builds seamlessly.
